# Delete your Kasafi data

*Last updated: 27 October 2026*

## On WhatsApp (quickest)

From the number you used with Kasafi, send:

> **GOGE BAYANAI NA** or **DELETE MY DATA**

Kasafi will reply to confirm.

*Hausa:* Aika **GOGE BAYANAI NA** zuwa Kasafi a WhatsApp daga lambar da ka yi amfani da ita. Za mu goge saƙonninka
da amsoshinmu, kuma Kasafi zai tabbatar maka.

## By email

Write to [bilyaminu@halalnumeriq.com.ng](mailto:bilyaminu@halalnumeriq.com.ng) and include the WhatsApp number you
used. We may ask you to confirm the number is yours by sending a message from it.

## What we delete

Your messages, voice-note transcripts, Kasafi's replies and evidence images, and your phone number and WhatsApp name
from our records. We do this within **7 days**, and usually straight away.

## What we may keep

- Records that no longer identify you. For example: "a question about the 2025 education budget could not be answered".
- Anything the law requires us to keep. If that applies, we will tell you.

## What we can't delete

Messages on your own phone and in WhatsApp stay there until you delete them yourself: open the Kasafi chat, tap
Kasafi's name, then *Clear chat*. WhatsApp's privacy policy covers how WhatsApp handles your data.

You have the right to deletion under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023**. If you are not satisfied, you can complain
to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** at [ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng).
See also the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
