# Kasafi Terms of Use

*Last updated: 27 October 2026*

Kasafi is a WhatsApp assistant run by **Halal Numeriq Limited**, Sokoto, Nigeria. By messaging Kasafi you agree to
these terms.

## What Kasafi does

Kasafi answers questions about **public documents**, such as Sokoto State budgets, budget performance reports,
policies, plans and reports from development partners. It cites the document and page it used, and sometimes sends an
image of the source table.

## What Kasafi does not do

- It gives **no opinions on politics**, politicians, parties, elections or how the government is performing. It only puts
  facts from documents side by side.
- It does not cover **conflict or security** topics.
- It gives **no medical or nutrition advice**. Health budget figures and statistics are fine. **In a medical emergency,
  go to the nearest health facility immediately.**
- It is **not legal, financial or official government advice**, and Halal Numeriq is not part of the government.

## Accuracy

Kasafi is in a **pilot stage** and uses AI, which can make mistakes, including mishearing voice notes. Always check
important figures against the cited document before relying on them. The documents themselves come from their
publishers, and we are not responsible for errors in them.

## Fair use

Please don't use Kasafi to send spam or illegal content, to harass anyone, or to try to break or misuse the service.
We may stop responding to numbers that do.

at the top shows the latest version. These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
