In short

  • Kasafi answers questions about public government documents, such as Sokoto State budgets, plans and reports.
  • We use your messages only to answer you. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • The AI services that read your question get the question only. They never get your phone number or WhatsApp name.
  • We delete voice recordings once they have been turned into text. Conversations are deleted after 90 days.
  • You can ask us to delete everything at any time: $contact.

1. Who we are

Kasafi is run by Halal Numeriq Limited, a company in Nigeria. We are the data controller for the personal data described here, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). To contact us about privacy, $contact.

2. What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number and profile name. WhatsApp gives us these with every message. We need the number to send you a reply.
  • What you send us: text messages and voice notes.
  • What we send back: answers, the documents and pages we cite, and images of the source tables.
  • Technical records: times, message types, errors and how long answers took. We keep these without your full phone number.

We do not ask for your real name, ID number, address or location, and you do not need to give them.

3. Please do not send sensitive personal details

Kasafi answers questions about public documents. Questions such as "how much was the 2026 health budget?" are fine. Please do not send details about your own health, or anyone else's, or other private matters. If you do, we use them only to reply, and we delete them under the same rules as the rest of your conversation. Kasafi does not give medical advice. In a medical emergency, go to the nearest health facility immediately.

4. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

  • To answer your questions: this includes turning voice notes into text, searching documents and sending replies. Legal basis: your consent, which you give by messaging Kasafi. You can withdraw it at any time by stopping, or by asking us to delete your data.
  • To keep Kasafi accurate and safe: this includes reviewing questions we could not answer and fixing mistakes. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a reliable service. Before review, questions are separated from your phone number.
  • To meet legal duties where the law requires it.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not build profiles of your political or other views. Kasafi gives neutral facts from public documents only.

5. Who processes your data

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries messages between you and Kasafi. WhatsApp's own handling of your data is covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Meta (Meta Model API) and Baseten run the AI models that transcribe voice notes and help write answers. They receive your question (the audio or text) and document extracts. They never receive your phone number or WhatsApp name. While Kasafi is in its pilot stage, it uses Meta developer credits, and under Meta's terms the content sent with those credits may be used by Meta to improve its products. This is another reason not to include personal details in your questions.
  • Hosting providers run our server and database: a private server, and Microsoft Azure.

Some of these providers process data outside Nigeria. Where personal data leaves Nigeria, we rely on the providers' data protection terms and the transfer rules of the NDPA.

6. How long we keep it

  • Voice notes: we delete the audio as soon as it has been turned into text.
  • Conversations (your messages and our replies): 90 days, then deleted.
  • Technical logs: 30 days. Logs do not contain the text of your messages or your full phone number.
  • Unanswered questions kept to improve Kasafi are stored without your phone number.

7. Your rights under the NDPA

You have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this page);
  • get a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • have wrong data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (see how to delete your data);
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use your data;
  • get your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, $contact. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

8. Security

All traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Incoming messages are checked to make sure they really come from WhatsApp, and only authorised staff can access the systems. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the NDPC as the NDPA requires and tell the people affected.

9. Children

Kasafi is meant for adults. Anyone under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
