Delete your Kasafi data

Last updated: $updated · Halal Numeriq Limited, Nigeria

The quick way

Send GOGE BAYANAI (or DELETE MY DATA) to Kasafi on WhatsApp, from the number you used with Kasafi. Kasafi will reply to confirm.

Other ways to ask

You can also $contact. If you write to us from somewhere other than WhatsApp, tell us the WhatsApp number you used so we can find your data. We may ask you to confirm that the number is yours by sending a message from it.

What we delete

  • All your messages and voice-note transcripts;
  • all of Kasafi's replies to you, and the evidence images we sent you;
  • your phone number and WhatsApp name from our records.

We delete this within 7 days of your request, and usually straight away. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to have your personal data deleted.

What we may keep

  • Records that no longer identify you, for example "a question about the 2025 education budget could not be answered". These have no phone number, name or message history attached.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep. If that applies, we will tell you.

What we cannot delete

Messages stay on your own phone and in WhatsApp until you delete them there. WhatsApp's own handling of your data is covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy. To remove the chat from your phone, open the Kasafi chat in WhatsApp, tap Kasafi's name, then Clear chat.

Questions or complaints: $contact. You can also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.
